# Sacramento Junior Roller Derby Brand Guide

This skill is a dedicated design and component guide for Sacramento Junior Roller Derby. Use it when building or refining web UI, page sections, navigation, event cards, team pages, or promotional content.

## Brand Personality

- Bold, athletic, and community-driven
- Classic roller derby grit with a modern, sporty polish
- Focused on high-energy events, local Sacramento pride, and accessible navigation
- Friendly but authoritative: clear calls to action, easy to scan, and strong team presence

## Visual Tone

- Strong contrast with dark, moody overlays over action photography
- Clean white typography for navigation and headings
- Direct, minimal layout with bold section labels and CTA buttons
- Sporty and approachable aesthetic with subtle texture or overlay effects

## Key Brand Colors

Use a restrained palette for web UI and components.

- Primary dark: `#0B233F` (nav bar, button backgrounds, overlays)
- Accent white: `#FFFFFF` (text, icons, logo, callouts)
- Secondary highlight: `#F2BF35` (action buttons, highlights, emphasis)
- Support gray: `#B9C2CC` (secondary text, dividers, captions)
- Dark accent: `#1F3A5B` (cards, dark section backgrounds)

## Typography

- Primary: Strong sans-serif with clean letterforms
- Use crisp uppercase / tracking for navigation, section headings, and buttons
- Use a layered approach: bold headings, clear body copy, and concise labels

Suggested hierarchy:

- Heading: `700` weight, uppercase or small caps for nav/section labels
- Subheading: `600` weight, clear and slightly condensed
- Body: `400` weight, easy reading with moderate line-height

## Layout and Components

### Navigation

- Use a horizontal menu with prominent links: Home, About, Events, Teams, Join, Sponsors, News, Contact
- Keep nav text white on dark background
- Provide a visible cart or utility icon in the top-right on pages with merch or shop support
- Keep spacing clean and evenly distributed

### Hero / Banner

- Use a full-width hero with action photography, dark blue overlay, and a strong white headline
- Place a short supporting statement or CTA button below the headline
- Use contrast and overlay so text remains legible on top of imagery

### Cards and Sections

- Use dark cards with white text for events, team profiles, and sponsorship spots
- Accent important details with the yellow highlight `#F2BF35`
- Keep card styling simple, with consistent padding and subtle border or shadow

### Buttons

- Primary button: dark blue background, white uppercase text
- Secondary button: white background with dark border and dark text
- Use bold labels like `Learn More`, `Buy Tickets`, `Join Now`, `Get Involved`

## Imagery and Icons

- Prefer roller derby action shots, team logos, skates, or event posters
- Use imagery with moody blue overlays and spotlights on athletes
- Keep iconography simple, bold, and sports-inspired
- Use circular or badge-style team elements when appropriate

## Copy Style

- Keep messaging direct and energetic
- Use short action-driven phrases
- Use Sacramento Junior Roller Derby-specific prompts: `Join Sacramento Junior Roller Derby`, `Upcoming Events`, `Our Teams`, `Support Local Derby`

## Developer Guidance

When creating web components or design systems, follow these rules:

1. Build components with consistent spacing and dark backgrounds.
2. Use white text for primary navigation and headers.
3. Use `#F2BF35` for CTA highlights, badges, and important status labels.
4. Avoid overly decorative fonts: keep the interface athletic and legible.
5. Use hero banners, event cards, and team tiles to support community energy.

## Suggested Component Patterns

- `srd-hero-banner`: full-width hero with overlay, headline, subtext, and CTA button
- `srd-nav-bar`: sticky top navigation with white links and highlight states
- `srd-event-card`: dark card with event title, date, and CTA button
- `srd-team-tile`: team tile with logo, team name, and quick action link
- `srd-footer`: contact + social links + simple mailing list or sponsor link

## Use Cases

- Creating web components for event announcements or schedules
- Styling team pages, roster sections, and sponsor showcases
- Designing responsive navigation and hero sections for the site identity
- Writing UI copy for Sacramento Junior Roller Derby promotional content
